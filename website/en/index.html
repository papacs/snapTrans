One gesture from screen to meaning.

snapTrans compresses capture, recognition, translation and reading into one natural action.

  1. 01

    Press the shortcut

    Alt + Q by default. A reliable text selection is read directly; otherwise, screen selection opens.

  2. 02

    The translation streams into the original region. Copy it, reverse the direction, or keep working.

The essential flow

Local recognition, streaming translation

RapidOCR extracts text on your PC. Only recognized text goes to the model provider you choose. DeepSeek, LiteLLM and other OpenAI-compatible APIs are supported.

↗

Capture and mark up

Common questions

A few things worth knowing.

Do I need an API key?

Not for capture or local OCR. Online translation needs a key for DeepSeek, LiteLLM or another OpenAI-compatible provider. Your provider may charge separately.

Does the model receive my screenshot?

No. RapidOCR recognizes the screenshot locally and snapTrans sends only the resulting text for translation.

Is macOS supported?

The current release target is Windows 10/11. A Mac version is planned, but capture permissions, shortcuts and secure key storage require native adaptations.

When will it reach Microsoft Store?

MSIX packaging, privacy materials and Store certification are being prepared. Email us for launch updates.
